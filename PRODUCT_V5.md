# Neta Telegram v5 — product freeze

## Product loop
**Daily → Neta Memory → Progress → Return**

Neta is not positioned as a question bank. The differentiator is memory:
the system keeps the learner's answer history, identifies weak skills and mixes
new questions with useful repetition in short daily sessions.

## Business funnel
1. Acquisition source / referral.
2. Three-question value demo.
3. First personalized Daily.
4. Reminder opt-in.
5. Repeat learning days and streak.
6. Progress / skill map.
7. Friends and channel for organic retention.
8. Honest Neta School interest signal.
9. D1/D3/D7 + funnel + latency/error reporting in admin analytics.

## Product rules
- No forced diagnostic before value.
- No fake NMT score from a tiny sample.
- No forced channel subscription.
- No fake reviews, scarcity or course availability.
- No Premium sales pressure in the active learner UX.
- Existing paid rights and payment history stay intact.
- Leaderboard is opt-in, pseudonymous and capped at one point per day.

## Visual language
Use a restrained set of recurring markers rather than decorative spam:
⚡ Daily, 🧠 Memory, 🎯 accuracy, 🔥 streak, ↻ repetition, 👥 friends,
📣 channel, 🎓 School.

This creates recognizable hierarchy without turning every answer into a sticker wall.

## Freeze criterion
After the live v5 smoke-test, do not add features merely because they sound useful.
Change the Telegram product only when retention, funnel, support reports or errors
show a concrete problem. Product development should then move primarily to Neta Web.
